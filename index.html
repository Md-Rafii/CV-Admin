Expiry & retention policy

Premium expiry: disable Premium-only access; do not erase core financial records.

  • 15-day grace/renewal window.
  • Provide visible export/backup before any eligible deletion.
  • Never delete entire account, personal wallet, core transactions, receipts or business records merely due to expiry.
  • Delete only explicitly designated temporary Premium-only data under a published policy, after notice and documented recovery/export window.
  • Account deletion must be a separate confirmed user request and follow legal retention obligations.
